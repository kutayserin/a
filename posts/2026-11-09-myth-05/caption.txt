“You cannot apply in Italy without a Dichiarazione di Valore”

The truth: CIMEA documents are also an option. For your diploma, the circular says CIMEA (equivalence center) documents or a Dichiarazione di Valore from the consulate. The university chooses, so ask first.

Source: unife.it, poliba.it, univr.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
