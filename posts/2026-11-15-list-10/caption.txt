Published IELTS minimums in the USA

Columbia: 7.5
Cornell: 7.5 (Academic)
Caltech: 7.0 (7.0 in each section)
Rice: 7.0 (Academic; General not accepted)
UC Berkeley: 6.5
UT Austin: 6.5 (Academic)
Univ. of Washington: 6.0

These are minimums for bachelor's applications. Harvard, Stanford, NYU and Northwestern do not publish a minimum. A minimum is not a guarantee of admission.

Source: undergrad.admissions.columbia.edu, admissions.cornell.edu, admissions.caltech.edu, admission.rice.edu, admissions.berkeley.edu, admissions.utexas.edu, admit.washington.edu, college.harvard.edu, admission.stanford.edu, nyu.edu, admissions.northwestern.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
