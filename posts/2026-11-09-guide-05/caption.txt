Applying on Common App: a five-part summary

1. Gather your documents: High school transcript, activities, test scores if you have them, family details. Your school counselor sends the School Report.
2. Open an account, add schools: You can open an account any time. The 2026-27 season has more than 1,200 member institutions.
3. Invite your recommenders: Each university sets its own recommendation requirements: counselor, teacher, sometimes a coach or employer.
4. Essays: Personal essay: required at some schools, optional at others. There may also be school-specific questions and a writing supplement.
5. Submit: Each university has its own deadline. Testing policy (required or optional) also varies by school.

The UC system uses its own application. UT Austin accepts both Common App and ApplyTexas.

Source: commonapp.org, admission.universityofcalifornia.edu, admissions.utexas.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
