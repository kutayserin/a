York master's fees: £27,840 to £34,500 by subject

2027/28 international taught master's: many MAs £27,840, economics £28,200, computing £33,900, finance £34,500. The law LLM at £14,390 is an exception. York has one fee table for EU and international students.

Source: york.ac.uk (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
