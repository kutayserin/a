Princeton: SAT optional for fall 2027

At Princeton, the SAT/ACT is optional for fall 2026 and 2027 entry and required again for fall 2028. You need two teacher references and one counselor reference. If your schooling is not in English, a language test is required, no minimum. Regular deadline: 1 January.

Source: admission.princeton.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
