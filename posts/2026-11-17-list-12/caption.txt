Need the SAT for fall 2027? It depends on the university

Harvard: Required (limited exceptions)
Yale: Required (SAT or ACT)
MIT: Required (SAT or ACT)
Stanford: Required (fall 2026 and later)
Princeton: Optional (required again in fall 2028)
Duke: Optional (2026-27 application cycle)
UCLA: Not considered (UC system)

Policies change year by year. Check the page for the cycle you are applying in. Not required does not mean your score is useless.

Source: college.harvard.edu, admissions.yale.edu, mitadmissions.org, admission.stanford.edu, admission.princeton.edu, admissions.duke.edu, admission.ucla.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
