IELTS or TOEFL, and what the new TOEFL score means

1. TOEFL has changed: Since 21 January 2026, TOEFL iBT uses a 1-6 scale with half bands. A section average replaces the old 0-120 total.
2. Equivalents: B2 = 4 and 4.5, C1 = 5 and 5.5, C2 = 6. For example, band 5 matches 95-106 on the old scale.
3. Read the report: For two years, the ETS report shows CEFR, the 1-6 score and the old equivalent together. The single scale starts in 2028.
4. What universities ask for: Berkeley 4.5, Imperial Standard 4.5, Tilburg 5 for September 2027. Some institutions, like Rietveld, only take IELTS.

TOEFL is not on the approved test list (SELT) for a United Kingdom visa. SOAS recommends IELTS for UKVI.

Source: ets.org, admissions.berkeley.edu, imperial.ac.uk, tilburguniversity.edu, rietveldacademie.nl, soas.ac.uk (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
