Annual fees at public universities: by income and country

Bologna (minimum): €157.04 (ISEE €27,000 or below, first year, 2026/27)
Sapienza Rome: €300-€1,500 (Türkiye group €700; confirm 2027/28)
Verona: €1,000 (flat option for non-EU without ISEE)
Bozen-Bolzano: about €1,200 (non-EU, two installments)
Turin: €156-€2,800 (one program; 2025-26 range)

The fee depends on ISEE (a family income indicator) or a country list. The upper limit varies by program. Ask the university about extra charges.

Source: unibo.it, uniroma1.it, univr.it, unibz.it, apply.unito.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
