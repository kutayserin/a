At Wageningen, your speaking score counts too

WUR master's: IELTS overall 6.5, speaking at least 6.0; TOEFL iBT 90, speaking 22 (new scale 4.5 and 4.0). You must get the scores in a single test. The non-EU master's fee for 2027/28 is €22,560. Bachelor's requirements may differ.

Source: wur.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
