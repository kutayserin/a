Berkeley: TOEFL 90, UC application by 30 November

For English, Berkeley asks for TOEFL 90, or 4.5 on the new scale, or IELTS 6.5. UC applications for fall 2027 run from 1 October to 30 November 2026. The UC system has no early round: one application, multiple campuses. Scores by 31 January.

Source: admissions.berkeley.edu, admission.universityofcalifornia.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
