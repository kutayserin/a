Imperial has two English levels

2026/27: Standard level is IELTS 6.5 (6.0 in each section) or TOEFL 92 / new scale 4.5. Higher level is IELTS 7.0 (6.5) or TOEFL 100 / 5.0. Which level applies depends on the program. Check your program's page.

Source: imperial.ac.uk (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
