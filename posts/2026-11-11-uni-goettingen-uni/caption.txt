Göttingen: limited-place bachelor's deadline is 30 April

For non-EU applicants, winter semester bachelor's applications open on 1 March: programs with limited places close on 30 April, open admission on 30 September. Master's and PhD dates vary by program. The page gives no year; confirm for 2027.

Source: uni-goettingen.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
