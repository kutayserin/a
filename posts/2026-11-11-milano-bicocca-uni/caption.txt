Milano-Bicocca's English requirement: IELTS 5.5

Non-EU applicants applying to English-taught programs on a visa need B2: IELTS Academic 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 72. From 2027/28 there are entry requirements by country; check the list for Türkiye.

Source: en.unimib.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
