Bocconi's 2027/28 master's fee is now clear

The first-year fee on Master of Science programs is €19,300. Partial waivers can bring it down to €15,440 or €7,720. There is no EU/non-EU distinction. The bachelor's fee is different. Waivers are granted through merit and need assessment.

Source: unibocconi.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
