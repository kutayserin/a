Hanze: deadline for Turkish students is 1 June

At Hanze, the non-EU deadline is 1 June for September entry and 1 November for February entry. 1 May is only for seven countries; Türkiye is not on the list. Selective programs have other dates; immigration steps come earlier. No year given; confirm for 2027.

Source: hanze.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
