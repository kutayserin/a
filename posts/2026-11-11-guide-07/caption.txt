In Italy, your income sets your public university fee

1. What is ISEE: Family income and assets indicator. At Bologna, €27,000 or less pays only a €157.04 installment in the first year (2026/27).
2. Income abroad: If your income is in Türkiye, you need separate documents instead of ISEE. Some institutions charge a fixed fee by country.
3. Examples: At Sapienza, the Türkiye group pays €700 (bands €300, €700, €1,500). At Verona, a fixed €1,000 for non-EU students without ISEE.
4. Timing: Bologna 2026/27: first installment €157.04 for bachelor's on 24 September 2026; second 18 December, third 15 March 2027.

The cap depends on the program. Ask the university about extra costs.

Source: unibo.it, uniroma1.it, univr.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
