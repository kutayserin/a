uni-assist and VPD: the way to apply to some German universities

1. Check the university: Not every university uses uni-assist. The university tells you whether it asks for a VPD (pre-assessment document).
2. Open a My assist account: Create your online application and upload your diploma and transcript. The university tells you which documents you need.
3. Pay the fee: First program €75, each extra one in the same semester €30. Payment requests only come through the uni-assist portal.
4. Wait for the VPD: Processing usually takes 4 to 6 weeks. Apply at least 8 weeks before the deadline. A VPD is valid for one year.
5. Upload to the university: Send the VPD and your other documents to the university's own portal, before its own deadline.

Some universities take applications directly; at others, uni-assist forwards them. The fee applies again each semester.

Source: uni-assist.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
