The Hague: English-taught master's €14,000

At The Hague UAS, the general non-EU fee is €10,528 and a full-time English-taught master's is €14,000 (2026/27). Non-EU applications close on 1 May. Your file must be complete by 15 June. 2027 dates have not been updated yet.

Source: thuas.com (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
