Irvine's yearly bill for out-of-state students: $59,375

An out-of-state bachelor's student starting in fall 2026 pays $59,375 in 2026-27. Of that, $39,270 is the out-of-state surcharge and $3,214 is health insurance. International students pay this too. Fall 2027 rates may differ. Housing is separate.

Source: reg.uci.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
