“University in Germany is completely free”

The truth: Most places: no tuition, but a semester contribution. No tuition at most universities, but a contribution every semester: FU Berlin €376.80, Münster €369.50 (winter 2026/27). Rent and living costs are separate.

Source: fu-berlin.de, uni-muenster.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
