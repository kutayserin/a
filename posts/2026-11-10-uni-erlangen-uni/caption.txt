Erlangen: a new rule from summer 2027

From the 2027 summer semester, FAU will charge non-EU students tuition on some programs. Those starting in winter 2027/28 are also covered. New enrollments per semester: bachelor's €1,000-3,000, master's €2,000-6,000. The list is on the FAU page.

Source: fau.eu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
