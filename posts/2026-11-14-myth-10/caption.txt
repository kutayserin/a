“I can always apply to the Netherlands until 1 May”

The truth: Some institutions set an earlier non-EU date. Numerus fixus closes on 15 January. At Tilburg, Rotterdam UAS and Aeres, the deadline for non-EU applicants is 1 April. 1 May is only the general date.

Source: studyinnl.org, tilburguniversity.edu, rotterdamuas.com, aeresuas.com (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
