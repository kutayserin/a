One semester's contribution at FU Berlin: €376.80

FU Berlin has no tuition. The 2026/27 winter semester contribution is €376.80, of which €226.80 is the semester public transport ticket. The summer 2026 amount was €358.80; the figure is updated every semester.

Source: fu-berlin.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
