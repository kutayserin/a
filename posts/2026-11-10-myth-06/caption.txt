“State university fees in Italy are the same for everyone”

The truth: Fees are based on family income. The fee depends on ISEE (a family income indicator). In Bologna, an ISEE of €27,000 or below pays €157.04 in the first year (2026/27). Sapienza charges a fixed fee by country.

Source: unibo.it, uniroma1.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
