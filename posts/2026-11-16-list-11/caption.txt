After 21 January 2026, your TOEFL score runs from 1 to 6

Caltech: 5 (100 on the old scale)
Twente (September 2027): 5 (100 on the old scale)
Tilburg (September 2027): 5 (4.5 for February 2027 entry)
Imperial Standard: 4.5 (92 on the old scale)
UC Berkeley: 4.5 (90 on the old scale)
UT Austin: 4 (79 on the old scale)

On the new scale, the overall score is the average of the four sections. Until 2028, ETS also shows the old equivalent on score reports. Confirm with the university.

Source: ets.org, admissions.caltech.edu, utwente.nl, tilburguniversity.edu, imperial.ac.uk, admissions.berkeley.edu, admissions.utexas.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
