Diploma document for Italy: CIMEA or Dichiarazione di Valore?

1. There are two routes: The circular recognizes two routes: CIMEA (equivalence center) documents or the Dichiarazione di Valore. The university chooses.
2. What universities say: Bari Polytechnic and Verona accept both. At Florence, you need one before enrollment (2024/25 page).
3. For visa-exempt applicants: Turkish passport holders are not in this group. The circular recommends not asking visa-exempt applicants for the Dichiarazione.
4. Allow time: Request documents early. For high school, the visa file needs the original diploma and, if required, an Italian translation.

In Lisbon Convention countries (Türkiye included), the CIMEA comparability document can be downloaded from the ARDI site.

Source: unife.it, poliba.it, univr.it, unifi.it, en.unimib.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
