Genoa: no Italian exam for English-taught programs

English-taught programs at Genoa need no Italian test. For 2026/27, applicants who need a visa applied from 26 November 2025 to 20 March 2026. 2027/28 dates are not out yet. Last year some programs closed early. Application fee €30.

Source: unige.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
