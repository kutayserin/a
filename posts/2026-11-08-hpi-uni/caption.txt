HPI's computer science master's has a short window

HPI takes winter semester applications from 1 April to 1 June. International applicants also need the GRE General Test. APS (diploma verification certificate) is only for diplomas from India, China and Vietnam.

Source: hpi.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
