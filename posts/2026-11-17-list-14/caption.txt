Non-EU? In the Netherlands, the deadline is not 1 May everywhere

Numerus fixus: 15 January (programs with limited places, 23:59)
Tilburg master's: 1 April (September 2027 entry)
Rotterdam UAS: 1 April (non-EU bachelor's and autumn master's)
Aeres UAS: 1 April (non-EU; applications open on 1 October)
Breda UAS: 1 May (bachelor's; master's 1 June)
Hanze UAS: 1 June (September entry; February entry 1 November)

Most pages do not state a year. The dates repeat each year, but confirm 2027 with the university. The general Studielink date is 1 May.

Source: studyinnl.org, tilburguniversity.edu, rotterdamuas.com, aeresuas.com, buas.nl, hanze.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
