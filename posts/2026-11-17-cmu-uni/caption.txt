Carnegie Mellon 2026-27 tuition: $69,702

Carnegie Mellon's flat bachelor's tuition for 2026-27 is $69,702 ($969 per unit). Fees and living costs are separate. There is no EU distinction in the USA. Estimated total cost for a first-year in a dorm is $93,614.

Source: cmu.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
