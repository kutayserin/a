How numerus fixus programs work in the Netherlands

1. What it is: Programs with limited places. You apply through Studielink by 15 January, 23:59 (CET).
2. Selection process: The institution makes its selection between 15 January and 15 April. On 15 April you see your ranking number in Studielink.
3. Limits: At most two numerus fixus programs a year. Only one application in medicine, dentistry, dental hygiene and physiotherapy.
4. The offer: Accept within two weeks or the place goes to someone else. With fewer applicants than places, everyone gets an offer on 15 April.

The general date for other programs is 1 May. Institutions may set earlier dates for non-EU applicants.

Source: studyinnl.org (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
