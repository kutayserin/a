Bachelor's applications through UCAS: one form, key dates

1. Open an account, add choices: Applications for 2027 entry opened on UCAS on 12 May 2026. You apply to more than one university with a single form.
2. Know the deadlines: Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, dentistry, veterinary closed 15 October 2026. Most programs: 13 January 2027, 18:00 (UK time).
3. If you are late: Extra (an added choice) opens on 25 February. Applications received by 30 June 2027 are forwarded, then it's Clearing.
4. Over the summer: Clearing (applying for unfilled places) opens on 2 July 2027. The deadline is 23 September 2027.

Master's applications go directly to the university, not through UCAS, and dates vary by program.

Source: ucas.com (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
