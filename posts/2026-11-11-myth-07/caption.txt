“Turkish students also need an APS certificate for Germany”

The truth: The uni-assist list names three countries. In the uni-assist glossary, APS (a diploma verification certificate) is required only for China, India and Vietnam. Türkiye is not mentioned. Ask the university too.

Source: uni-assist.de, tu-dresden.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
