TUM fees rise in winter 2027/28

TUM charges non-EU master's students €4,000 or €6,000 per semester. From winter 2027/28, new enrollments pay €4,600 or €6,800. Bachelor's: €2,000/€3,000, new enrollments €2,300/€3,400. Depends on the program; contribution is separate.

Source: tum.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
