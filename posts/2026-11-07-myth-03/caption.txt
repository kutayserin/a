“University in the Netherlands is €2,771 a year”

The truth: That fee is for EU passports. The €2,771 statutory fee (2027/28) is for EU/EEA passports. On a Turkish passport, you usually pay the institutional fee: Delft master's €26,479, Erasmus €22,800.

Source: tudelft.nl, eur.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
