Groningen's master's IELTS requirement varies by program

2027/28: most master's IELTS 6.5 (TOEFL 90 or 4.5); some law and linguistics and many research master's 7.0 (100 or 5). Literary Studies C2. There are sub-score requirements. The non-EU master's fee starts at €19,900, medicine €32,000 (2026/27).

Source: rug.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
