Applying on Studielink in four steps

1. Find the program: Pick the program on Studyfinder and check the application route on the institution's page. Some use a different route.
2. Register on Studielink: Open an account, add your past education and submit. The institution may then ask for more documents on its own portal.
3. Watch the dates: Numerus fixus closes on 15 January at 23:59; the general date is 1 May. Institutions may set earlier dates for non-EU applicants.
4. Accept the offer: Numerus fixus rankings come out on 15 April. If you don't accept the offer within two weeks, the place goes to someone else.

Apply to at most two numerus fixus programs a year; in fields like medicine, one application a year to that program.

Source: studyinnl.org (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
