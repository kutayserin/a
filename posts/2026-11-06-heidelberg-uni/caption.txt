Heidelberg: €1,500 per semester for Turkish students

In Baden-Württemberg, non-EU/EEA students pay €1,500 tuition per semester for a bachelor's and a master's in the same field. That is €3,000 a year. The semester contribution is paid separately. EU/EEA citizens do not pay this fee.

Source: uni-heidelberg.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
