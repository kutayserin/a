How pre-enrollment for the visa works in Italy

1. Get admitted first: Complete the university's own application. Universitaly pre-enrollment doesn't replace admission; the university approves.
2. Pre-enroll on Universitaly: Fill in the pre-enrollment form on the ministry's portal (no fee). The university approves the application digitally.
3. Meet the language requirement: At least B2 for bachelor's and master's. For foreign-language programs, the visa needs a B2 certificate covering four skills.
4. Go to the consulate: Apply with your approved pre-enrollment summary, diploma, equivalence document (CIMEA or Dichiarazione di Valore) and proof of funds.
5. Don't miss the dates: Bachelor's and master's: apply for the visa by 31 October 2027. No enrollment by 31 January 2028 means the visa is cancelled.

2026/27 and 2027/28 circular. Universities may set their own earlier dates.

Source: unife.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
