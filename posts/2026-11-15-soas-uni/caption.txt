You need TOEFL 95 for direct entry at SOAS

SOAS direct entry requirement: IELTS 6.5 (6.0 in all sub-scores) or TOEFL iBT 95 (writing 23, others 20). On the new scale, 5.0. TOEFL is not on the approved test (SELT) list for the visa. IELTS for UKVI is recommended for the visa.

Source: soas.ac.uk (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
