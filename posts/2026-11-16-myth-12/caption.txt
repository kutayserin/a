“No school in the USA asks for the SAT anymore”

The truth: Many schools have made it required again. Harvard, Yale, MIT and Stanford ask for the SAT or ACT. It is optional at Duke and Vanderbilt. At Princeton it is optional in 2027 and required in 2028.

Source: college.harvard.edu, admissions.yale.edu, mitadmissions.org, admission.stanford.edu, admissions.duke.edu, admissions.vanderbilt.edu, admission.princeton.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
