Key dates for fall 2027 bachelor's applications

UC (all campuses): 30 November 2026 (one application from 1 October)
UT Austin: 1 December 2026 (Early Action 15 October)
Vanderbilt: 1 January 2027 (second early decision and regular decision)
Caltech: 4 January 2027 (regular decision; early round 1 November 2026)
Duke: 4 January 2027 (regular decision; early decision 2 November)

Early rounds are usually 1-2 November. Most universities use the Common App. UC uses its own system, and UT Austin accepts both.

Source: admission.universityofcalifornia.edu, admissions.utexas.edu, admissions.vanderbilt.edu, admissions.caltech.edu, admissions.duke.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
