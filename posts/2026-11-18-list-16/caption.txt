English thresholds for a master's in the Netherlands

Groningen (most): 6.5 (TOEFL 90 or new scale 4.5)
Groningen law, linguistics: 7.0 (TOEFL 100 or new scale 5)
Groningen Literary Studies: C2 (2027/28 page; no score given)
Twente: 6.5 (6.0 in each section)
Wageningen: 6.5 (speaking 6.0)
Tilburg: 6.5 (research master's 7.0)

There are sub-score requirements and exceptions. If you studied in English, you may be exempt.

Source: rug.nl, utwente.nl, wur.nl, tilburguniversity.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
