Plan backwards to start in September 2027

1. November-December 2026: UC applications close on 30 November. US regular applications: 1-5 January. For the UK, UCAS is 13 January 2027.
2. January-March 2027: Netherlands numerus fixus 15 January. Bocconi bachelor's Winter Session 26 January. RWTH master's 1 March.
3. April-June 2027: Netherlands non-EU dates fall between 1 April and 1 June. Germany winter semester: Dresden 31 May, HPI 1 June.
4. June-August 2027: Admission letters, proof of funds, visa appointment. You can get a US visa up to 365 days before classes start.
5. September 2027: Enrollment, orientation. In Italy, bachelor's and master's visa applications by 31 October 2027, enrollment by 31 January 2028.

These dates are examples. Your program's own page takes priority. Add 8 weeks for steps like the uni-assist document (VPD).
