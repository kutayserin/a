Twente's TOEFL threshold rises from September 2027

Master's: IELTS 6.5 (each section 6.0). TOEFL: 90 or 4.5 on the new scale for February 2027 entry; 100 or 5 from September 2027. The non-EU master's fee for 2027/28 ranges from €18,720 to €22,320 by program.

Source: utwente.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
