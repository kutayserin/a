Columbia's minimum: IELTS 7.5, TOEFL 105

Columbia asks for at least IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 105 for bachelor's applications. The page gives no new TOEFL scale equivalent, so confirm before you apply. Applicants with SAT reading and writing 700+ or ACT English or Reading section 29+ are exempt.

Source: undergrad.admissions.columbia.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
