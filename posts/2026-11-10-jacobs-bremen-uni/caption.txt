Private university in Bremen: €11,925 per semester

Constructor University's 2027/28 bachelor's semester fee is €11,925, the same for EU and non-EU. The one-off enrollment fee is €940 for non-EU students. That comes to about €23,850 a year; accommodation and meals are separate. Master's fees may differ.

Source: constructor.university (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
