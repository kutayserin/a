Minimum English requirement in Seattle: IELTS 6.0

University of Washington asks first-years for IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 76 (new scale 4.0). The deadline is 15 November; test by 31 December at the latest. Meeting the minimum does not mean admission. The page does not state a year.

Source: admit.washington.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
