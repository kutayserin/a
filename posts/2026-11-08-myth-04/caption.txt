“If you are an EU citizen, you pay home fees in the UK”

The truth: An EU passport does not get you home fees. As an EU citizen starting in 2026/27, you pay the same fee as international students unless you have UK residence status (settled/pre-settled).

Source: info.lse.ac.uk, york.ac.uk (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
