Non-EU bachelor's fees: from Delft to Rotterdam

TU Delft: €20,563 (BSc, institutional fee)
Erasmus Univ. College: €17,800 (non-EU institutional fee)
Leiden (lowest): €14,700 (science €19,200, medicine €31,400)
Erasmus Rotterdam: €14,700 (RSM €15,200, medicine €35,000)
Statutory fee (EU/EEA): €2,771 (EU/EEA passport holders)

2027/28 fees. At Leiden and Rotterdam they rise by subject. On a Turkish passport, the institutional rate usually applies.

Source: tudelft.nl, universiteitleiden.nl, eur.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
