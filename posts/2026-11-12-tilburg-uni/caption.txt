Tilburg: last day for September 2027 is 1 April

Non-EU master's deadline: 1 April for September 2027 entry, 1 October for February entry. IELTS 6.5 (writing and speaking 6.0) required. Exceptions: two research master's 1 March, Clinical Neuropsychology 15 January. New TOEFL: 5.
