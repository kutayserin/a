RWTH master's applications open on 1 December

For non-EU applicants, winter semester master's applications run 1 December to 1 March, and summer semester ones 1 June to 1 September. Limited-place deadlines: 15 July for winter, 15 January for summer. The page gives no year, so confirm.

Source: rwth-aachen.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
