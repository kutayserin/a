Bolzano's one chance for non-EU applicants: Early Bird

Non-EU applicants living abroad can only apply in the Early Bird round (usually March to April). The annual fee is about €1,200. Application fee €50. CIMEA (equivalence center) documents required at enrollment. The page gives no year.

Source: unibz.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
