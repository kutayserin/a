How to show €11,904 for a German visa

1. Know the amount: According to DAAD's Study in Germany portal, in most cases you need to show €11,904 for one year.
2. Choose a method: Blocked account (Sperrkonto), bank guarantee, commitment from someone living in Germany, family income or scholarship letter.
3. Open the account early: Apply for the blocked account early. Processing usually takes a week, and the confirmation goes in your visa file.
4. Check with the consulate: The German mission in your country decides which proof it accepts and the current amount.

The amount depends on estimated living costs and is updated. Confirm before you apply.

Source: study-in-germany.com (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
