Dresden: an earlier deadline for non-EU applicants

For the winter semester, non-EU applicants with foreign diplomas apply from 1 April to 31 May. With an EU or German diploma, the deadline is 15 July. Through uni-assist, first application €75. Some master's programs have different dates. No year is stated.

Source: tu-dresden.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
