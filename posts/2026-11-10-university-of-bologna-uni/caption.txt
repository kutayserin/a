Bologna's lowest annual fee is €157.04

The fixed installment at Bologna is €157.04. If your family's income indicator (ISEE) is €27,000 or less, in 2026/27 you pay only this amount in your first year. Later years have a credit requirement. No document means the full fee. The cap varies by program.

Source: unibo.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
