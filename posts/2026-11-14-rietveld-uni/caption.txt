Rietveld Academie does not accept TOEFL

For 2027/28, Rietveld accepts only IELTS Academic 6.0 (speaking 6.0); TOEFL is not valid. The foundation year portfolio is due 15 December 2026. Submit the IELTS certificate with your portfolio. Year 2 and 3 applications: 1 April 2027.

Source: rietveldacademie.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
