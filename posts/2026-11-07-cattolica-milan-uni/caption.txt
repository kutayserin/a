Cattolica scholarship: €6,400 instead of €9,350

2026/27 first year: non-EU students with income from abroad pay €9,350 for a master's, €8,800 for a bachelor's. A UCSC scholarship cuts the master's to €6,400. With EU income, at most €6,850. You apply for the scholarship; it is not automatic.

Source: international.unicatt.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
