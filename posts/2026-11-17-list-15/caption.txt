When do winter semester applications open in Germany?

RWTH Aachen master's: 1 December-1 March (non-EU, open admission; limited places 15 July)
Göttingen bachelor's: 1 March-30 April (limited places; open admission 30 September)
HPI master's: 1 April-1 June (GRE General Test required)
TU Dresden: 1 April-31 May (non-EU, foreign diploma)
Hamburg master's: 1 May-15 June (2026/27; international 15 February-31 March)

These windows recur and no year is stated. Confirm them for 2027/28. Add 8 weeks for the uni-assist preliminary review document (VPD).

Source: rwth-aachen.de, uni-goettingen.de, hpi.de, tu-dresden.de, uni-hamburg.de, uni-assist.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
