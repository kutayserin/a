Your passport's price at TU Delft: €2,771 or €26,479?

In 2027/28 the statutory fee is €2,771, but non-EU/EEA students pay the institutional fee: bachelor's €20,563, master's €26,479. If you apply on a Turkish passport, the institutional (non-EU) rate usually applies.

Source: tudelft.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
