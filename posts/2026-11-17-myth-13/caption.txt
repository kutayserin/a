“uni-assist is free and you only apply once”

The truth: You pay per program and per semester. First program €75, each extra program in the same semester €30. You pay again in a new semester. VPD (preliminary review) takes 4-6 weeks and is valid for one year.

Source: uni-assist.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
