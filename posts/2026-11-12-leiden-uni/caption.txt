Leiden's bachelor's fee doubles depending on the program

2027/28 non-EU bachelor's fee ranges from €14,700 to €31,400: science and biomedical €19,200, Leiden University College €21,120, medicine €31,400. Master's fees and IELTS requirements are set by program.

Source: universiteitleiden.nl (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
