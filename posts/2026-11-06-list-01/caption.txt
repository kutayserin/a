What's the semester contribution in Germany? Six universities

FU Berlin: €376.80 (winter 2026/27, transport ticket included)
Münster: €369.50 (winter 2026/27)
Bonn: €363.07 (winter 2026/27)
Bochum: about €350 (social contribution)
Freiburg: €190 (non-EU students also pay €1,500 tuition)
Stuttgart: €184 (non-EU students also pay €1,500 tuition)

Amounts are per semester, updated each semester, and exclude tuition. In Baden-Württemberg, non-EU students also pay €1,500.

Source: fu-berlin.de, uni-muenster.de, international.ruhr-uni-bochum.de, uni-bonn.de, student.uni-stuttgart.de, uni-freiburg.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
