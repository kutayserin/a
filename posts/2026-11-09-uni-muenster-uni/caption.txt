Münster: €369.50 semester contribution, no tuition

The University of Münster charges no tuition. The semester contribution for winter 2026/27 is €369.50, about €739 a year. Covers public transport too and is updated every semester. Rent and living costs are separate.

Source: uni-muenster.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
