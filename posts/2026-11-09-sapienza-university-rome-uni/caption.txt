State university in Rome: €300 to €1,500 a year

Sapienza charges non-EU students living abroad €300, €700 or €1,500 a year, depending on country of residence (2026/27). On the fee table, Türkiye is in group B: €700 a year (with income from abroad). Confirm 2027/28.

Source: uniroma1.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
