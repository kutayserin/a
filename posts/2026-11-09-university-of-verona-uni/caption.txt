Verona: a flat €1,000 option if you have no income certificate

Non-EU students who cannot submit ISEE, Italy's income certificate, can apply for a flat all-inclusive fee of €1,000 a year (2026/27). International master's applications for 2026/27 closed on 31 March; 2027/28 dates are not out yet.

Source: univr.it (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
