In this state, non-EU students pay €1,500 per semester

Heidelberg: €1,500 (per semester, non-EU/EEA)
KIT Karlsruhe: €1,500 (per semester, non-EU/EEA)
Freiburg: €1,500 (plus €190 semester contribution)
Stuttgart: €1,500 (plus €184 semester contribution)
Tübingen: €1,500 (per semester, non-EU/EEA)
Konstanz: €1,500 (per semester, non-EU/EEA)
Mannheim: €1,500 (per semester, non-EU/EEA)

State rule: €3,000 a year. The semester contribution is paid separately. EU/EEA citizens pay no tuition.

Source: mwk.baden-wuerttemberg.de, uni-heidelberg.de, uni-freiburg.de, student.uni-stuttgart.de, uni-konstanz.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
