“There is no tuition anywhere in Germany”

The truth: There are exceptions: Baden-Württemberg, TUM, FAU. Baden-Württemberg: €1,500 per semester for non-EU students. TUM master's: €4,000 or €6,000 (from 2027/28: €4,600 or €6,800). FAU will also charge from summer 2027.

Source: mwk.baden-wuerttemberg.de, tum.de, fau.eu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
