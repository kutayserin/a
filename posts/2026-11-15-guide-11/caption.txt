USA student visa: from admission to flight

1. SEVP-approved school: Your school must be in the US student program (SEVP). It enters you in SEVIS and issues Form I-20, your admission document.
2. I-901 SEVIS fee: Pay it on FMJfee.com before you apply for the visa. Your details must match your I-20 exactly.
3. Consulate interview: An interview is required for ages 14-79. Take your I-20 with you. The order of steps can vary by consulate.
4. Timing: The visa can be issued up to 365 days before your course starts. You can enter the USA no earlier than 30 days before.

If you got I-20s from more than one school, choose one for the visa. Your name and date of birth must match your passport.

Source: travel.state.gov, ice.gov (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
