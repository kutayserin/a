Harvard does not require TOEFL

For first-year applications to Harvard College, an English test is optional, not required. The SAT or ACT is required, with limited exceptions. Not required doesn't mean the score doesn't matter. The decision is entirely Harvard's.

Source: college.harvard.edu (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
