Student visa proof of funds: five countries, five rules

1. Germany: €11,904 for one year (DAAD). A blocked account is one option. Ask the consulate which one applies.
2. Netherlands: Dutch immigration service (IND) 2026 amount: €1,130.77 a month, valid until 31 December 2026.
3. Italy: 2026/27 and 2027/28 circular: €10,179.85 a year. The source must be legal, traceable and preferably your own or family money.
4. UK: 1 year of tuition + £1,529 a month (London) or £1,171, for 9 months; 28 days in your account. Visa £558, healthcare £776/year.
5. USA: No fixed amount on the official page. The school registers you in SEVIS and issues Form I-20. Visa issued up to 365 days before classes.

Amounts are updated during the year. Check the official page on the day you apply.

Source: study-in-germany.com, ind.nl, unife.it, gov.uk, travel.state.gov, ice.gov (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
