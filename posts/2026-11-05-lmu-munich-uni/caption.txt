No tuition on most programs at LMU

LMU has charged no tuition since winter 2013/14; you pay a semester contribution. Some special master's programs do charge fees. Munich's student services organization sets the contribution amount. Living costs are separate.

Source: lmu.de (checked November 2026). Confirm on the official page before you apply.
